# Kurung Anggun: ASMR example (HyperFrames)

24.5 s, 1080×1920, no voiceover. On-screen text in Bahasa Melayu, close-mic ASMR sound effects.

## Creative calls
- Character: the woman from the user's Gemini character sheets.
- Outfit recoloured to match the real product: the red/gold sheet became maroon with silver songket and a silver brooch; the songket on the cuffs was toned down to plain. Navy kept as is (navy with gold is correct). Black made from the navy sheet.
- Shots: hook on the model under an arch, then songket, front zip and brooch, bunga tabur, comfort (three-quarter turning to side), sizes, three colours, end card with the yellow-basket call to action.
- Sound: ElevenLabs free-plan sound effects: room tone, footsteps, fabric rustle, zip, taps, skirt swish. No music.

## What was checked
- `hyperframes check`: no errors or warnings.
- Snapshot review of every scene and cut. The sound was measured, not heard.

## Weak spots
- The sheets are small (1152×924), so the close-ups are soft. Higher-resolution sheets or product photos would sharpen them.
- Navy and black still show songket on the cuffs; they are on screen for about a second.
- Not built: the Hypit version, the voiceover version.
