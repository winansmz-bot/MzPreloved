**Length:** ~40s · **Format:** 9:16, talking head in a rounded card + motion graphics (style of the reference reel)
**Talking point:** 10 billion Pokémon cards were printed last year, yet Japan now has to ban selling *empty boxes* and wants ID for card lotteries — because of scalpers. So where's the line between collector and scalper?
