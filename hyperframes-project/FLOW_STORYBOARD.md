# Flow storyboard: HyperFrames explainer (14 clips)

Make each clip in Google Flow, then send all of them back. I'll join them in order, transcribe the new voice and re-time every caption, card and sound effect to match. You don't need to hit the times below exactly: they're only a guide to how much each clip says.

## Settings for every clip

- **Mode:** Frames to Video (image-to-video), with **your presenter photo as the first frame** of every clip, so she looks the same throughout
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 portrait
- **Length:** 8 s
- **Quality:** download at **1080p** (or upscale) if Flow offers it
- **Name the files** `clip01.mp4` to `clip14.mp4`

## Rules that make the clips join cleanly

1. **The camera is locked.** No zoom or pan. I add all camera moves and cuts afterwards.
2. **Start speaking at about 0.5 s and finish by about 7 s,** then hold a natural smile. That gives me room to cut.
3. **Same voice in every clip.** The voice description is identical in every prompt. Don't change it.
4. **No on-screen text, subtitles or music.** I add all graphics and captions here.
5. **Pronunciation:** "Claude" = *klod*, "HyperFrames" = *haiper-freims*, "CapCut" = *kep-kat*, "Canva" = *ken-va*, "HeyGen" = *hei-jen*. If Flow mispronounces a word, regenerate the clip, or tell me and I'll fix it in the captions.
6. Make **2 versions** of each clip and keep the one with the best mouth and steadiest face.
